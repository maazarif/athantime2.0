# AthanTime 2.0

A one-page prayer-times app with a live sky. The sun and moon sit where they really are in the sky, the moon shows its real phase, and the sky colour follows the sun.

**Design: "Horizon"**. The sky is the clock. Today's real sun path is drawn as a dotted arc with markers for sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr and Maghrib. The sun and moon sit where they actually are, and the moon shows its true phase. The next prayer is the headline, and within 30 minutes of it the countdown takes over.

**Features**
- Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, worked out on your device (no account, no tracking)
- The current prayer is in bold, with a countdown to the next one
- 13 calculation methods, Standard or Hanafi Asr, and high-latitude rules
- Auto-location or city search (via OpenStreetMap)
- An alert setting for each prayer (tap its bell): Bells, Chime, Silent or Off, plus a reminder 5, 15 or 30 minutes before
- Installable (add it to your Home Screen) and works offline

**Deep links**: `#settings` opens Settings and `#alert-fajr` opens the Fajr alert.

**Preview links**: `?lat=47.61&lng=-122.33&name=Seattle&at=2026-11-15T17:30` pins a location and time, which is handy for testing the sky.
